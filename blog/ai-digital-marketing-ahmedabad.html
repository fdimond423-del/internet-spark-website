AI in Digital Marketing: How to Use ChatGPT for Content Creation

Revolutionizing Content Creation in Ahmedabad

Artificial Intelligence is no longer a futuristic concept; it's here, and it's changing the way businesses in Ahmedabad market themselves. From tech firms on SG Highway to traditional manufacturing units in Odhav, companies are looking to scale their content efforts.

At Internet Spark, Ahmedabad's most innovative content marketing agency, we use AI to enhance—not replace—human creativity. Here are our workflows for using ChatGPT effectively.

1. Content Ideation and Strategy

Don't ask ChatGPT to write an article from scratch. Instead, use it for ideation. Prompt it with your target audience (e.g., "Homebuyers in Ahmedabad") and ask for 10 unique angles or content gaps that your competitors have missed.

2. SEO Outline Generation

We use AI to reverse-engineer top-ranking pages. By feeding ChatGPT the top 3 articles for a keyword, we ask it to generate a comprehensive outline that is better structured and more informative.

3. Social Media Copy & Ad Variations

A/B testing is crucial for Meta Ads. We prompt ChatGPT to create 10 different hooks and ad copies, some in professional English and some with a local Hinglish or Gujarati touch, which highly resonates with the Ahmedabad demographic.

Conclusion

AI is a powerful assistant, but the strategic direction must come from experts. To see how AI-driven content can scale your brand, check out our content marketing and SEO services.
