E-commerce Marketing: How We Scaled from ₹10L to ₹2Cr Monthly Revenue

The Blueprint for D2C Success in Ahmedabad and Beyond

Scaling a direct-to-consumer (D2C) brand requires more than just running a few ads. It requires a holistic, data-driven approach. At Internet Spark, Ahmedabad's leading E-commerce marketing agency, we recently took a local fashion brand from ₹10 Lakhs to over ₹2 Crores in monthly revenue.

Here is the exact framework we used to achieve a 20X growth for this Ahmedabad-based brand.

1. Fixing the Funnel & CRO

Before scaling ad spend, we optimized the Shopify store. Improving page load speed, simplifying the checkout process, and implementing highly convincing product descriptions increased the Conversion Rate (CR) from 1.2% to 3.5%.

2. Omni-Channel Performance Marketing

We didn't rely on just one platform. We deployed a full-funnel strategy using Meta Ads for top-of-funnel discovery, Google Shopping Ads to capture high intent, and retargeting across YouTube and Display networks.

3. Retention over Acquisition (Email & WhatsApp)

Acquisition costs are rising globally. By implementing robust Klaviyo email flows and automated WhatsApp retention campaigns, we increased the Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) by over 60%, drastically improving overall profitability.
